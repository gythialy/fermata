The name

A note held longer.

Fermata is the musical sign that tells a player to hold a note past its written value. The orchestra waits; then it goes on. 停琴, "the qin stops", comes from a line by the Southern Qi poet Xie Tiao. Both names describe the same pause; the moon in the icon comes from that line — the qin put down, a moment in the cool moonlight.

「停琴伫凉月，灭烛听归鸿」
The qin stops; he stands in the cool moonlight, blows out the candle to hear the returning geese.
Xie Tiao · 谢朓 《移病还园示亲属诗》

The rhythm

25 / 5, four times, then 15.

Pomodoro by default: a 5-minute mini break every 25 minutes, and a 15-minute long break after every four mini breaks. Both kinds are adjustable, and at least one stays on.

What it does

Quiet where it counts.

01

A field, not a dialog

One window per display at 85% of the screen: a crescent moon, one suggestion, a hairline that fills with the break, and exactly one exit.

02

Strict when you mean it

No postponing, no skipping, no closing the window; the app refuses to quit until the break ends. A locked screen still ends it.

03

A pause that knows

30 minutes, an hour, until morning, or indefinitely. Or by itself: away, in Focus, while the screen is locked, and for the apps you exclude.

04

A due break that waits

A fullscreen app holds the break up to an hour; typing holds it up to 30 seconds. Then it starts with its full duration.

05

Settings that stay quiet

General, Schedule, Appearance, About. English or 简体中文, start at login, menu bar style, field colour, sounds, app exclusions.

06

Light on the machine

One Go process with a native menu bar item and no webview. Idle CPU near zero; nothing to keep in memory.
