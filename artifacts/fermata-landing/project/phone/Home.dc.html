The name

A note held longer.

Fermata is the sign that holds a note past its written value. 停琴, "the qin stops", comes from a line by Xie Tiao. Both describe one gesture.

「停琴伫凉月，
灭烛听归鸿」
The qin stops; he stands in the cool moonlight.
